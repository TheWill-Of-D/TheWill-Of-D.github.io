About

I’m Dhanoosh Pooranakumaran, a PhD student at NTU Singapore.

I think about physical intelligence: how robots can interact with unfamiliar objects, reason under uncertainty, and acquire only the information that matters to a decision.

My current questions sit at the intersection of robot learning, probabilistic world models, active perception, and learning across embodiments. I am particularly interested in what a robot should do when the correct action cannot be inferred from observation alone.

This website is where I work through those ideas in public. My first research note asks why robots must learn what to measure—and when they know enough to act.
